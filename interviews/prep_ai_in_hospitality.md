# Interview Prep: AI in hospitality

## Questions

- How has AI changed the way you interact with guests or customers in your hospitality role?
- What's been the most surprising thing you've discovered about using AI technology in your day-to-day operations?
- Can you walk me through a specific situation where AI either saved the day or completely missed the mark?
- What aspects of hospitality do you think should never be automated, and why?
- How do your guests typically react when they realize they're interacting with AI versus a human team member?
- What's one AI application in hospitality that you think is overhyped, and one that's actually underrated?
- If you had unlimited budget and resources, what AI solution would you implement tomorrow to improve your guests' experience?
- Looking ahead five years, what worries you most about AI's role in hospitality, and what excites you most?
